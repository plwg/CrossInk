inki

Anki decks for your CrossInk reader

1Your Anki export

In Anki, choose File › Export, pick Anki Deck Package and tick Include scheduling information so your progress comes along. (An Anki Collection Package works too.)

2Put a deck on the reader

Download a deck file and copy it into the flashcards folder on the SD card. Subdecks are included.

Choose your Anki export first.

3Add new cards to a deck on the reader

Added cards in Anki? Take the deck's .inki file from the SD card's flashcards folder, choose it here together with a fresh export in step 1, and copy the downloaded file back over the old one. Progress made on the reader is kept. Don't study on the reader in between.

4Scheduling optional

Train FSRS-7 on the review history in your export so intervals fit how you remember, then copy fsrs7-params.txt into the flashcards folder. Without it the reader uses the defaults.
